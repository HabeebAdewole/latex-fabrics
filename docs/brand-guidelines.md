BRAND STORY & FOUNDATION
Who we are, what we stand for, and why it matters

## 1.1  About Latex Fabrics

Latex Fabrics is a premium fabric enterprise founded and owned by Mrs. Adewole Latifat, with over two decades of experience in the wholesale and retail distribution of high-quality textile materials. Operating from two flagship locations in Oshodi, Lagos — 447 Agege Motor Road, Bolade Bus Stop, and Shop 3E, Kairo Market — the business has built an enduring reputation for authenticity, variety, and luxury.

The company serves a diverse and loyal clientele including fashion designers, boutique owners, event planners, and individual customers who seek premium fabrics for life's most important occasions — weddings, owambe celebrations, traditional ceremonies, and everyday elegance.

Latex Fabrics carries an extensive collection of exquisite materials: Swiss voile, cord lace, tissue lace, cordnet lace, dry lace, cordnet sequins, cordlace sequins, and other luxurious textiles sourced from trusted international suppliers.

## 1.2  Mission Statement

| To make premium African and international fabrics accessible to every Nigerian — from the master tailor to the everyday customer — through an elegant, trustworthy, and seamless shopping experience. |
|---|

## 1.3  Vision Statement

| To become Nigeria's most trusted digital fabric destination, known for quality, cultural pride, and exceptional customer service. |
|---|

## 1.4  Brand Values

| Luxury | Premium presentation, packaging, and experience at every customer touchpoint — online and offline. |

## 1.5  Brand Positioning

Latex Fabrics sits at the intersection of cultural authenticity and modern luxury. We are not a generic online fabric store. We are the digital extension of a trusted Lagos institution — a business that has dressed brides, clothed celebrations, and supplied the city's finest tailors for over twenty years.

Our positioning statement:

VISUAL IDENTITY
The visual language that communicates our brand at a glance

## 2.1  Visual Identity Principles

Every visual element of Latex Fabrics should feel like it belongs in the same world — warm, rich, cultural, and premium. The design language draws from West African textile traditions: the warmth of kente gold, the depth of earthy mahogany tones, the softness of ivory linen.

- Warm over cold — always prefer warm tones over clinical blues or stark whites

- Texture over flatness — convey richness, even on digital surfaces

- Restraint over excess — premium brands do not shout; they command attention quietly

- Culture-forward — Nigerian context should be visible in every design decision

## 2.2  Overall Aesthetic Direction

| Dimension | Direction |
|---|---|
| Style | Traditional African Luxury — rich cultural heritage meets modern elegance |
| Mood | Warm, refined, celebratory, trustworthy |
| Reference points | High-end Nigerian fashion brands, luxury African textile houses |
| Avoid | Generic e-commerce feel, cold minimalism, western fast-fashion aesthetics |

## 3.4  Accessibility & Contrast

All text and interactive elements must meet WCAG 2.1 AA contrast requirements. Minimum contrast ratio of 4.5:1 for normal text, 3:1 for large text and UI components. Always verify Heritage Gold (#C9A84C) contrast before using it as text color — it is approved only on Deep Mahogany backgrounds.

TYPOGRAPHY
Fonts, sizes, weights, and hierarchy

## 4.1  Type Families

| Font | Role & Notes |
|---|---|
| Playfair Display | Display font — hero sections, store name presentations, full-page headings. Conveys luxury and editorial authority. Google Fonts, free. |
| Georgia | Heading serif — H1, H2, document titles, pull quotes. Built-in system font, always available. |
| Lato | Body & UI font — all body text, buttons, labels, navigation, forms, captions. Clean, readable, versatile. Google Fonts, free. |
| Fallback stack | Georgia → 'Times New Roman' → serif for headings. Lato → 'Helvetica Neue' → Arial → sans-serif for body. |

## 4.2  Type Scale

| Role | Specification |
|---|---|
| Display / Hero | Playfair Display · 700 Bold · 48–72px · Letter spacing: +2px · Hero sections only |
| H1 — Page Title | Georgia · 700 Bold · 36–40px · Letter spacing: +1px · One per page |
| H2 — Section Title | Georgia · 600 · 28–32px · Section headings |
| H3 — Sub-heading | Lato · 600 SemiBold · 22–24px · Card titles, component headings |
| H4 — Label | Lato · 700 Bold · 16–18px · All caps, +3px tracking · Field labels, tags, badges |
| Body — Regular | Lato · 400 Regular · 16px · Line height 1.7 · All descriptive text |
| Body — Small | Lato · 400 Regular · 14px · Line height 1.6 · Captions, metadata, timestamps |
| Price | Lato · 700 Bold · 18–24px · Heritage Gold · Product prices always bold |
| CTA / Button | Lato · 700 Bold · 15–16px · All caps · +2px letter spacing · All button text |
| Tagline | Georgia · 300 Light Italic · 14–16px · +2px tracking · Tagline use only |

## 4.3  Typography Rules

- Never use more than 2 font families in any single layout

- Never set body text below 14px

- Never use Playfair Display for body text — display fonts only at large sizes

- Headings should always have more visual weight than the content below them

- Line length (measure) for body text: 60–75 characters maximum for readability

- Left-align body text; center-align only for short hero headlines and taglines

- Use real typographic apostrophes and quotation marks — never straight quotes

LOGO USAGE
How to use, place, and protect the Latex Fabrics mark

## 5.1  Logo Versions

| Version | When to Use |
|---|---|
| Primary Logo | Full horizontal lockup — icon mark + 'LATEX FABRICS' wordmark. Use wherever space allows. This is the preferred version. |
| Stacked Logo | Icon mark above wordmark — use when horizontal space is limited (e.g., square social profile images). |
| Monogram Mark | 'LF' ligature or initial mark — for favicon (16×16, 32×32), app icon, embossed materials, and brand stamps. Never as the sole identifier in marketing. |
| Wordmark Only | 'LATEX FABRICS' text without icon — for contexts where the icon reduces too small to read (e.g., very long narrow banners). |
| Reversed Logo | White / Ivory Linen version for use on Deep Mahogany or dark photographic backgrounds. |

## 5.2  Clear Space

The logo must always be surrounded by a minimum clear space equal to the height of the capital 'L' in 'LATEX' on all four sides. No other graphic elements, text, imagery, or edges of the medium should appear within this exclusion zone.

## 5.3  Minimum Sizes

| Logo Version | Minimum Size |
|---|---|
| Full horizontal logo | Minimum 160px wide on screen · Minimum 45mm wide in print |
| Stacked logo | Minimum 80px wide on screen · Minimum 25mm wide in print |
| Monogram mark | Minimum 24px × 24px on screen · Minimum 8mm × 8mm in print |

## 5.4  Approved Color Variants

- Heritage Gold mark on Deep Mahogany background — primary preferred version

- Heritage Gold mark on Ivory Linen background — secondary preferred version

- Deep Mahogany mark on Ivory Linen background — tertiary, for minimal contexts

- White / Ivory Linen mark on photographic backgrounds — when placed over imagery

- Single-color black mark — approved for print contexts where color is unavailable

## 5.5  Logo Misuse

The following are never acceptable under any circumstances:

| Do not stretch, squash, or distort the logo in any dimension Do not rotate or tilt the logo Do not apply drop shadows, glows, gradients, or decorative effects Do not place the logo on a busy patterned or photographic background without a clear overlay Do not recolor the logo outside the approved color variants above Do not recreate the logo in a different font Do not add outlines, strokes, or borders to the logo mark Do not place the logo below the minimum size thresholds Do not use the monogram mark alone as the primary brand identifier in marketing materials Do not crowd the logo — always maintain clear space |
|---|

06
TONE OF VOICE & MESSAGING
How Latex Fabrics speaks and what it says

## 6.1  Voice Overview

Latex Fabrics communicates with warmth, confidence, and cultural pride. The brand never talks down to its customers — it speaks with them, like a trusted fabric expert who genuinely wants them to find the right material.

The voice is consistent across every channel — the website, WhatsApp conversations, social media captions, product descriptions, and any printed material. A customer who interacts with Latex Fabrics on Instagram and then walks into the store should feel like they are meeting the same brand.

## 6.2  Voice Dimensions

| Voice Quality | How It Shows Up |
|---|---|
| Knowledgeable, not academic | We share expertise naturally — like a trusted friend who knows fabrics deeply, not a lecturer. |
| Warm, not casual | We are approachable and human, but we maintain the elegance our brand represents. |
| Confident, not arrogant | We know our products are excellent. We state this clearly without boasting. |
| Cultural, not exclusive | We reference Nigerian life, occasions, and fabric traditions without alienating anyone. |
| Clear, not simplified | We respect our customers' intelligence. No condescension, no over-explanation. |

## 6.3  Writing Guidelines

- Use clear, everyday Nigerian English — professional but never stiff

- Reference occasions and context: 'Perfect for your Owambe', 'Ideal for aso-ebi coordination'

- Describe texture, weight, and drape — help the customer feel the fabric through the screen

- Be specific about quality: 'Swiss voile with a crisp, lightweight drape' beats 'high quality fabric'

- Always write prices in Nigerian Naira with the ₦ symbol: ₦12,500 not N12500 or NGN 12500

- Use second person ('you', 'your') in product descriptions and CTAs — speak directly to the customer

- Avoid generic phrases: 'premium quality', 'best in class', 'world class' — show, don't tell

- Never use ALL CAPS for emphasis in body copy — use bold text or restructure the sentence

## 6.4  Key Brand Phrases

These phrases are part of the Latex Fabrics voice and should be used consistently:

| Context | Approved Phrase |
|---|---|
| Tagline | Woven in Heritage. Draped in Luxury. |
| WhatsApp CTA | Order via WhatsApp |
| Quality assurance | Trusted by Lagos's finest tailors for over 20 years |
| Product intro | Discover our collection of [fabric type] |
| Order confirmation | Your order has been received. We'll respond within a few hours. |
| Offline catalog | Browse our full catalog — even without internet. |

## 6.5  Tone by Channel

| Channel | Tone Direction |
|---|---|
| Website / App | Polished, descriptive, confident. Product descriptions should be rich with fabric detail. CTAs are clear and direct. |
| WhatsApp | Warm, conversational, helpful. Like texting a knowledgeable vendor you trust. Use complete sentences — not fragments. |
| Social Media | Celebratory and cultural. Lean into occasions — wedding seasons, owambe, Sallah, Christmas. Show the fabrics in context. |
| Customer Service | Patient, empathetic, solution-focused. Never defensive. Every complaint is an opportunity to build trust. |
| Product Labels | Concise and precise. Fabric name, type, origin, price. No fluff. |

## 6.6  Do and Don't Examples

| ✅  'This Swiss voile drapes beautifully and stays crisp through a full owambe — perfect for that statement gele moment.' ✅  'Our cord lace is sourced directly from trusted international suppliers — the same quality Lagos's top designers rely on.' ✅  'Order via WhatsApp and Mrs. Adewole will personally help you choose the right yardage for your event.' |
|---|

| ❌  'Best quality fabric! Buy now! 100% authentic! Don't miss out!!!' ❌  'We offer a wide range of high quality premium world class fabric materials for all your needs.' ❌  'Hi dear, pls kindly check our catalog and let us know what you want thanks' |

DIGITAL PRODUCT GUIDELINES
Design and behavior standards for the Latex Fabrics PWA

## 7.1  Design Principles

- Mobile-first — the primary experience is a Nigerian customer on an Android phone, possibly on 3G

- Performance is a brand value — a slow app is a poor brand experience. Target sub-3 second load on Fast 3G

- Offline capability is not optional — customers may browse in areas with unreliable connectivity

- Every interaction should feel intentional and premium — no generic UI patterns

- WhatsApp is the hero checkout flow — make it prominent, never hidden

## 7.2  UI Component Standards

| Component | Specification |
|---|---|
| Navigation Bar | Deep Mahogany background · Heritage Gold logo · Ivory Linen navigation links · Gold cart badge · Sticky on scroll |
| Product Cards | Ivory Linen background · Rounded corners (12px) · Fabric type badge in Heritage Gold · Price in bold Heritage Gold · Full-width Add to Cart button |
| Buttons — Primary | Heritage Gold background · White bold text · 8px border radius · 48px minimum touch target |
| Buttons — WhatsApp | WhatsApp Green background · White bold text · WhatsApp icon · Used exclusively for order initiation |
| Buttons — Outline | Transparent background · 2px Heritage Gold border · Heritage Gold text · Secondary actions only |
| Input Fields | Ivory Linen background · 1px Muted Brown border · Charcoal text · Focus state: 2px Heritage Gold border |
| Category Filters | Pill shape (full border radius) · Inactive: Muted border · Active: Heritage Gold background, white text |
| Toast Notifications | Deep Mahogany background · White text · Bottom-center · 3 second auto-dismiss |
| Product Image | 4:3 aspect ratio · WebP format · Lazy loaded · Pinch-to-zoom on detail page |
| Price Display | Always in Heritage Gold · Lato 700 Bold · ₦ prefix · Never approximate (show exact price) |

## 7.3  PWA Requirements

- Installable on Android and iOS home screens via Web App Manifest

- Service worker caches all product data, images, and static assets on first load

- Offline mode: full catalog browsable without internet connection

- 'Add to Home Screen' prompt displays after the user's second visit

- Lighthouse PWA score must be 90+ before any public launch

- App icon: LF monogram mark in Heritage Gold on Deep Mahogany, all required sizes provided

- Splash screen: Deep Mahogany background with Heritage Gold logo centered

- Theme color in manifest: Deep Mahogany (#3D2B1F) — sets browser UI chrome color

## 7.4  Performance Standards

| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| First Contentful Paint | < 2.0 seconds on simulated Fast 3G |
| Largest Contentful Paint | < 3.0 seconds on simulated Fast 3G |
| Core bundle size | < 200KB gzipped |
| Image format | WebP with JPEG fallback — never PNG for product photos |
| Image loading | Lazy loading on all below-fold images |
| Lighthouse Performance | Score 85+ on mobile |
| Lighthouse Accessibility | Score 90+ — WCAG 2.1 AA compliance |
| Touch targets | Minimum 44×44px on all interactive elements |

## 7.5  Recommended Tech Stack

| Layer | Technology |
|---|---|
| Frontend Framework | React + TypeScript — type safety, component reuse, strong ecosystem |
| Styling | Tailwind CSS — rapid UI with consistent design tokens |
| State Management | Zustand — lightweight cart and wishlist state |
| Routing | React Router v6 — client-side navigation |
| PWA | Vite PWA Plugin — generates manifest and service worker automatically |
| Backend | Node.js + Express + TypeScript — consistent language across stack |
| Database | PostgreSQL with Prisma ORM — type-safe, relational, scalable |
| Image Storage | Cloudinary or AWS S3 — optimized delivery, automatic WebP conversion |
| Frontend Hosting | Vercel — free tier, automatic deploys from GitHub, excellent PWA support |
| Backend Hosting | Railway or Render — managed Node.js + Postgres, free tier available |
| Authentication | JWT tokens — admin dashboard access only; no customer accounts in V1 |

PHOTOGRAPHY & IMAGERY
Visual standards for product and lifestyle photography

## 8.1  Photography Principles

- All imagery must feel authentically Nigerian — Lagos context, local models, real occasions

- Never use generic Western stock photography — it undermines the brand's cultural authenticity

- Lighting should be warm and golden — evocative of premium fabric and natural light

- Fabric texture must be clearly visible — customers need to feel the material through the image

- Images should evoke aspiration — the customer should see themselves in the photo

## 8.2  Product Photography Standards

| Attribute | Standard |
|---|---|
| Background | Dark mahogany wooden surfaces, neutral stone, or Ivory Linen fabric — never plain white |
| Lighting | Warm directional light showing fabric texture and sheen — never flat studio lighting |
| Angles | Primary shot flat-lay or 45° showing full fabric; secondary shots showing texture close-up |
| Scale reference | Include a hand or common object in at least one shot to convey scale |
| Color accuracy | Colors must be accurately represented — customers make purchasing decisions based on these |
| Minimum resolution | 2000×1500px for catalog; 800×600px minimum for web display |
| Aspect ratio | 4:3 for all product catalog images — enforced at display level |
| File format | Master files as JPEG at 85% quality; serve as WebP with JPEG fallback |

## 8.3  Lifestyle Photography

- Nigerian women and men in elegant, culturally-appropriate attire made from Latex Fabrics materials

- Occasion contexts: weddings, owambe celebrations, traditional ceremonies, church, formal events

- Models should represent the diversity of Lagos — various skin tones, ages, and body types

- Settings: Lagos homes, event venues, outdoor celebration contexts — never foreign or ambiguous locations

- Close-up texture shots: fabric draped over hands, fabric in motion, fabric against skin

- Behind-the-scenes: tailors working with the fabrics, the Oshodi store, fabric displays

## 8.4  Image Don'ts

| Do not use white or grey seamless paper backgrounds for product photography Do not use heavy filters or presets that alter fabric color accuracy Do not use watermarks on product images — they reduce perceived quality Do not use images with visible brand marks from competitors Do not use overly edited or artificially enhanced fabric textures Do not use non-Nigerian cultural contexts (e.g., American or European settings) |

How the Latex Fabrics brand shows up in WhatsApp

## 9.1  WhatsApp as a Brand Channel

WhatsApp is not just a checkout tool for Latex Fabrics — it is a primary brand touchpoint. For most Nigerian customers, the WhatsApp conversation with Mrs. Adewole is the brand experience. Every message sent from the Latex Fabrics number must feel consistent with the premium, warm, knowledgeable brand voice defined in Section 06.

## 9.2  Business Profile Standards

| Element | Standard |
|---|---|
| Profile Photo | LF monogram mark on Deep Mahogany background — same as app icon |
| Business Name | 'Latex Fabrics' — no abbreviated forms, no emojis |
| Business Category | Shopping & Retail |
| Description | Premium fabric materials — lace, voile, sequins & luxury textiles. Wholesale & retail. Oshodi, Lagos. ✅ Verified supplier. |
| Away Message | Thank you for reaching out to Latex Fabrics. We're currently unavailable but will respond within a few hours during business hours (Mon–Sat, 8am–6pm). |

## 9.3  Auto-Generated Order Message Format

When a customer taps 'Order via WhatsApp', the following pre-filled message structure is generated. This format must remain consistent:

| Hello! I'd like to place an order from Latex Fabrics: 🧵  [Product Name] Quantity: [X] yards Price: ₦[price]/yard  ×  [X] yards  =  ₦[total] [Additional items follow same format] ──────────────────── Cart Total: ₦[grand total] My name: [Customer enters before sending] Delivery preference: [Pickup / Delivery — Customer selects] Please confirm availability. Thank you! 🙏 |
|---|

## 9.4  Response Tone Standards

- Always greet the customer by name if known: 'Hello Adaeze, thank you for your order!'

- Confirm receipt within 2 hours during business hours — set this expectation clearly

- Use warm, professional language — not overly casual, not stiff corporate speech

- Always confirm fabric availability before accepting payment

- If an item is unavailable, proactively suggest alternatives from the catalog

- Close every conversation with a warm sign-off: 'Thank you for choosing Latex Fabrics 🙏'

- Never use abbreviations like 'pls', 'ur', 'hw much' — write in full sentences

- Emojis are acceptable in moderation — maximum 1–2 per message, never in product details

MISUSE & COMMON MISTAKES
What to avoid to protect brand consistency

## 11.1  Visual Misuse

| Using pure white (#FFFFFF) backgrounds instead of Ivory Linen (#F5F0E8) Using Heritage Gold as body text color on light backgrounds (insufficient contrast) Using WhatsApp Green for anything other than the WhatsApp order button Using fonts other than Playfair Display, Georgia, and Lato Using gradient backgrounds — the brand palette uses flat, solid fills only Using stock photography that does not reflect Nigerian culture or context Placing the logo over busy backgrounds without a protective clear zone Scaling the logo below minimum size thresholds Using more than 2 font families in a single layout |
|---|

## 11.2  Voice & Copy Misuse

| Using generic superlatives: 'best', 'premium', 'world-class' without specific evidence Using overly casual WhatsApp language in official brand communications Writing prices without the ₦ symbol or with incorrect formatting Using ALL CAPS for emphasis in body copy Describing fabrics in vague terms ('nice fabric', 'good quality') instead of specific attributes Responding to customers in abbreviations or broken sentences Using Western cultural references in marketing material directed at Nigerian customers |
|---|

## 11.3  Digital Misuse

| Changing button colors away from the approved palette Reducing touch target sizes below 44×44px on mobile Using PNG format for product images instead of WebP/JPEG Disabling the PWA offline functionality Using the WhatsApp Green color anywhere outside the WhatsApp order button Placing multiple competing CTAs at equal visual weight on the same page Showing loading states that exceed 3 seconds without progress feedback |
|---|
